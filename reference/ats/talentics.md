# Talentics

Talentics careers pages: `<co>.talentics.app/` lists the jobs, each at `/jobs/<uuid>`; "APPLY FOR THIS JOB" opens `/jobs/<uuid>/apply` in place. No account, no CAPTCHA seen. The confirmation is the same URL with "Application submitted!" in the page. First measured 14 Oct 2026, on a Turkish employer whose own site linked it from its HR page, after an aggregator listing carried no apply link.

- **The form renders a few seconds after the URL changes.** A `querySelectorAll('input')` straight after the click returned nothing while the screenshot already showed the fields; wait about 4 s and read again.
- **Upload the CV first; it autofills everything.** `input[type=file][name=file]`; `file_upload` through the `read_page` ref works. The parse takes 10-20 s: wait until the hidden `file_id` and `parsed_resume_uuid` have values. It then fills `linkedin_profile`, `name`, `email`, `phone`, `current_company`, the hidden `total_years_experience` and its visible `<select>`.
- **The parser also builds a work-experience and education list, and gets the months wrong.** Every date came back as January (`01/2025 - 01/2026` for April to March) and an end date it could not read stays `MM/YYYY`. The list is plain spans (`.additional-info-item-placeholder`): clicking a date span opens four `<select>`s (month, year, month, year) inside that item, and setting them with `.value` plus `change` sticks; clicking the title turns it into a text input. Correct the dates to the profile's before submitting: this list is what the recruiter reads.
- **Native setter works** on the text inputs and the `cover_letter` textarea. Custom questions are `linkQuestion_<id>` text inputs; read each label from its parent, since the `*` on the label is not reflected in `required`.
- **Consent boxes are `checkbox_<id>`, labelled by the employer.** On the measured tenant: one acknowledges the candidate privacy notice, the other gives explicit consent to process and transfer the application data to domestic and foreign service providers for candidate selection. Both are the application's own data consents, not marketing: tick them only when the profile consents to application data processing.
