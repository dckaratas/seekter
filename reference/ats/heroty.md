# Heroty

VBT Yazılım's own HR product; employers using it publish postings at `app.heroty.com/jobs/<id>` and link them from their careers page. First seen 7 Oct 2026.

- **Postings stay readable after they close.** The page renders the full description with a "Şimdi Başvur" button, and only the click reveals a modal "Hata · Bu ilan aktif değil". Aggregators (whatjobs-tr via freehire) keep listing such postings for months. Click the button before reading anything else.
- The first click by `ref` did nothing visible and froze the screenshot for 30 s; a coordinate click on the button opened the modal.
- No form was reached yet, so the fields and whether an account is needed are unmeasured.
