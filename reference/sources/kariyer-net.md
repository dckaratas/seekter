# Kariyer.net

Turkey's largest general job board. First measured 6 Oct 2026, logged out.

- **Search URL:** `https://www.kariyer.net/is-ilanlari/<city-slug>?ct=<city-id>&kw=<keywords>` (Ankara is `ankara?ct=6`); without a city, `https://www.kariyer.net/is-ilanlari?kw=<keywords>`. **Slug-style keyword URLs (`/is-ilanlari/ankara-java-developer`) ignore the keyword** and return the city's whole list, sponsored rows first. Typing in the search box and pressing Return also did nothing; the "İş Ara" button produces the `?ct=&kw=` URL above.
- **Cards:** `a[href*="/is-ilani/"]`; the link text carries title, company, city, work model (`İş Yerinde` / `Hibrit` / `Uzaktan / Remote`) and age. Rows prefixed `Sponsorlu İlan` are ads that ignore the keyword. Rows marked `accessible` are roles reserved for candidates with a disability.
- **Details:** measured 6 Oct 2026, `fetch('/is-ilani/<slug>')` returned the full page HTML logged out. **Measured 9 Oct 2026, logged in, it returned a 7 KB `__NUXT__` shell with no posting text.** Navigate to each posting, wait about 5 seconds and read `document.body.innerText`; the requirements usually follow `İş İlanı Hakkında` and the employer's own criteria sit under `Aday Kriterleri` (Tecrübe, Yabancı Dil).
- **Search pages are rendered in the browser.** Measured 9 Oct 2026: `fetch('/is-ilanlari/ankara?ct=6&kw=java')` returned 200 and zero cards, while navigating to the same URL showed 19. Navigate once per query, wait a few seconds, then harvest; keep the harvest in `sessionStorage`, which survives the navigation on the same origin.
- **Search is not a title search.** `kw=backend` also matched company names that begin with the same letters in the local language and returned nurses, meter readers and call-centre staff. Filter cards on the profile's `title_keep` before reading them. `kw=node.js` returned 2 cards where `kw=node` returned 16 (10 Oct 2026): drop the punctuation. Adding a work-model word to `kw` (the local word for "remote") cut the results to 0-3 cards: search without it and read `Uzaktan / Remote` off the card instead.
- **The list has no date order and repeats.** On the third daily run in one city, eight of the nine on-title cards were already in the tracker. Expect most of a day's list to be dedup hits.
- **Volume:** one city, six keywords, three-day freshness not available: 98 unique rows, 8 on-title, most of them .NET or on-site.
- **Applying needs a Kariyer.net account** (the "Başvur" button asks to log in; a Google sign-up modal opens on first load: never accept it). Logged out, every fit is a hand-off. When the user is logged in to Kariyer.net in the same Chrome, the run applies through the site's own Başvur button (the login is theirs; Seekter never types a password).
- Cookie banner: "Reddet" at the bottom.

## Applying (logged in), measured 7 Oct 2026

- "Başvur" on the posting opens `/basvuru-tamamlama/<jobCode>`: three steps. **Özgeçmiş** uses the CV stored in the user's Kariyer.net profile (no upload). **Ön Yazı** is optional and defaults to "Ön yazı eklemek istemiyorum". With `tailor_cv` on, this step is where the tailoring goes, since the CV itself is the account's (`reference/ats/tailored-cv.md`); Measured 13 Oct 2026: "Yeni Ekle" opens "Ön Yazı Ekle" with a name field (`input[name=name]`, 50 characters) and a Quill editor (5000 characters; `.ql-container.__quill.setText()` fills it). The name needs real typing after a coordinate click on the field; measured 24 Oct 2026, JS `focus()` followed by typed text left the value empty, while a click at the field then typing filled it. "Kaydet" saves the letter to the account's list, dated, and selects it for this application (`input[name=selected-cover-letter]` checked); the account keeps it afterwards, so name it after the company and role. **Şirket Soruları** are the employer's questions; an employer without questions gets a two-step form (`(1/2)`, `(2/2)`) and "Başvurunu Tamamla" sits under the cover letter list.
- **Answers are pre-filled from the account's saved answers** (`Şirket Sorularım`), including ones from earlier applications and ones the user saved long ago. Read every pre-filled value against the profile before submitting; a value the profile does not cover (travel restriction, shift/weekend work) is not verified just because the site filled it.
- Dropdowns are custom widgets: coordinate-click the field, then the option. English scale: Başlangıç / Orta / İyi / İleri / Ana Dil. Years scale: 0-1 / 1-3 / 3-5 / 5-7 / 7+.
- **Some employers add a consent box under their questions** (measured 9-10 Oct 2026): "Şirkete verilerimi ve ilettiği sorulara yanıtlarımı işlemesi için açık rıza veriyorum", with the note that the employer only sees the answers when it is ticked. It is the application's own data consent: tick it only when the candidate's profile gives consent for application data, otherwise hand off. Group-company accounts (one Kariyer.net employer posting for several subsidiaries) also say the data may be shared within the group, so treat every subsidiary posted under one group account as the same company for the one-application-per-window rule.
- Measured 9-10 Oct 2026: questions not covered by the profile appear among the pre-filled saved answers, e.g. "Aktif olarak araç kullanıyor musunuz?" (actively driving). A pre-filled value there is the account's old answer, not a verified one.
- "Başvurunu Tamamla" lands on `/basvuru-onay?jobCode=…&applyCode=…`: that page is the confirmation. Answers stay editable for 48 hours under Başvurularım.
- The `ref` click on "Başvur" sometimes does nothing; a coordinate click works. Navigating straight to `/basvuru-tamamlama/<jobCode>` skips the button.
- **Some postings are reserved for candidates with a disability and say so only at submit.** Measured 10 Oct 2026: an agency's Java posting with no disability wording in its title, slug or text refused "Başvurunu Tamamla" with "Bu ilana sadece engelli adaylar başvurabilir". Nothing is sent; record it as a skip. Titles or slugs with `engelli` / `disabled` and cards marked `accessible` are the same thing and can be dropped before opening.
- **A Chrome window in the background breaks coordinate clicks here** (measured 9-10 Oct 2026). When `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`, screenshots time out every other call, coordinate clicks land nowhere, and "Devam Et" does not open the questions. Retake the screenshot until one succeeds and click right after it. `button.click()` from JS on "Başvurunu Tamamla" submits even while hidden, so read the answers first, then submit by JS and check for `/basvuru-onay`.
- Clicking outside the form can open "Yarım kalan başvurunu kaydedelim mi?" (save the unfinished application). Neither "Sil" nor "Daha Sonra Başvur" is wanted: reload `/basvuru-tamamlama/<jobCode>` instead.
- **The salary field ("Aylık net ücret beklentiniz nedir?") can already hold the account's saved figure, and typing appends to it.** Measured 11 Oct 2026: a click plus typed digits turned a saved value into one three orders of magnitude larger. Read `.value` first; to change it, `select()` the input, press Delete, type, then read `.value` again before submitting.
- **Replies arrive on the site, not in the inbox.** Measured 12 Oct 2026: six days and 20 Kariyer.net applications produced no mail from kariyer.net at all. The status lives at `/tum-basvurular` (`/basvurularim` is a 404): each card reads "Başvurun <date> tarihinde iletildi" until the employer acts, then e.g. "Özgeçmişin Görüntülendi". An inbox sweep (`/seekter-log`) has to read that page too, or every Kariyer.net application looks unanswered.
- **A mandatory question can have no truthful option.** Measured 23 Oct 2026: "Üniversite kaçıncı sınıfsınız?" (which university year are you in) offered only 1-4, no graduate choice, on a senior full-stack posting. Picking a year would be false: leave the tab with everything else filled and hand off. The options are a custom list (`input.kds-input-field`) that renders only after a click on the field itself, not on its "Seç" placeholder.
- Measured 9-10 Oct 2026: some employers ask "Şehir Seçiniz" (preferred city) twice, pre-filled from saved answers; check them against the profile's location rules.
